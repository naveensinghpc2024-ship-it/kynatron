About Us

Built around the realities of the factory floor

Our story began with a familiar frustration: important measurements, captured by equipment that wasn’t ready for the heat, dust and power changes of real industrial work.

From a factory-floor problem to a connected system

Our engineers had seen the same challenge across factories: data loggers could be costly, unreliable and poorly suited to heat, dust and fluctuating power. When readings matter to production and quality, equipment has to work where the process does.

That experience led us to build rugged, accurate and practical data acquisition systems for industrial environments—not just laboratory conditions. The GM-DL Pro was an early step in that work.

Today, that same focus connects data loggers, wireless sensors, monitoring and control systems, and cloud-connected SCADA. Our teams in Hyderabad and Bangalore design and manufacture products close to the conditions and people they are built to serve.

How we turn those principles into practice

From environmental design to in-house testing, each choice starts with the demands of real industrial use.

Designed for Indian Conditions

Our products handle 45°C ambient heat, voltage fluctuations (170-260V), dust, and humidity — because that's what Indian factories look like.

72-Hour Burn-In Testing

Every single unit runs continuously for 72 hours at max load before shipping. If it fails in our lab, it never reaches your factory.

In-House: PCB to Final Assembly

Zero outsourcing. We design the PCB, write the firmware, machine the enclosure, and assemble products across our Hyderabad and Bangalore operations.

Lifetime Firmware Updates

Free firmware upgrades for life. As we add features (new protocols, better algorithms, security patches), your device grows with you.
