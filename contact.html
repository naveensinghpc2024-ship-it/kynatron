Questions that often come up

Some quick context before you decide how you’d like to move forward.

What's your typical lead time?
Standard configurations ship within 7–10 business days. Custom builds take 3–4 weeks depending on complexity and specifications.
Do you offer custom configurations?
Absolutely. Custom channel counts, sensor types, enclosure designs, and firmware modifications are our specialty. Tell us your application and we'll design a solution.
What warranty do you provide?
All products come with a 1-year comprehensive warranty covering hardware defects. Extended 5-year plans are also available.
Do you ship pan-India?
Yes — we ship across India and internationally. Domestic delivery takes 3–5 days via premium courier with tracking. On-site installation and commissioning available for bulk orders.
What support do you offer post-purchase?
Lifetime technical support via phone/email, free firmware updates, and annual calibration services at nominal cost.
Are your products compliant with regulatory standards?
Yes — 21 CFR Part 11 (pharma), HACCP (food) and calibration certificates are included with every unit.
Can I get a demo before purchasing?
Absolutely. We offer live online demos and can arrange on-site demonstrations for bulk requirements. Request a demo →
