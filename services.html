Start with the challenge. Build the right response.

From capturing a dependable reading to helping your team act on it, each service supports a step in the same journey.

Data Acquisition & Logging

First, capture the measurements your process depends on with accurate, reviewable data logging.

  • Multi-channel temperature monitoring
  • Custom sensor integration (TC, RTD, 4-20mA)
  • High-speed sampling up to 1 sec intervals
  • CFR 21 Part 11 compliant data formats
  • 50+ year retention without data overwrite

Industrial Monitoring Solutions

Then make readings easier to follow with live dashboards, trends and views tailored to your operation.

  • Live sensor dashboards (web-based, zero install)
  • Multi-zone temperature & humidity monitoring
  • Trend analysis and historical data viewing
  • Remote monitoring via WiFi/4G/Cloud
  • Custom KPI dashboards for management

Alarm & Control Systems

When conditions change, configurable alarms and control logic help the right people respond.

  • Programmable alarm thresholds (high/low/rate)
  • Multi-level alarm escalation (screen, buzzer, SMS, email)
  • Equipment ON/OFF control via relays
  • PID loop control for process regulation
  • Setpoint remote configuration

IoT & Cloud Platform

Connect devices and teams with cloud access, integrations and records that support day-to-day oversight.

  • MQTT / REST API for cloud integration
  • Embedded web server (HTML5 HMI)
  • Mobile-friendly dashboards
  • Cloud data backup & sync
  • Role-based access control & audit trail

Industrial Automation

Where a process needs more, we can design PLC, HMI and SCADA control around the equipment already in place.

  • PLC/SCADA system design & programming
  • HMI development & panel building
  • Process optimization & control loops
  • Legacy system upgrades & migration
  • Modbus, OPC-UA, Ethernet/IP protocols

Hardware Design & Manufacturing

And when the solution calls for custom hardware, firmware or enclosures, our team can take it from design through build and calibration.

  • PCB design & fabrication
  • Industrial enclosure design (IP66/IP67)
  • Firmware & embedded systems development
  • Assembly, testing & calibration
  • OEM & white-label manufacturing
